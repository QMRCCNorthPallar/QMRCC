About QMRCC

Community Service & Social Empowerment

Quaid-E-Millath Relief & Cultural Center (QMRCC) is a non-profit organization established to serve the underprivileged communities in North Pallar and surrounding areas. We believe in creating meaningful change through sustainable community development initiatives.

Our mission is to provide relief, support education, promote cultural values, and empower individuals through various social welfare programs. We focus on housing support through Baithurahma, healthcare assistance, educational initiatives, and emergency response services.

Our Mission

Our Mission

Making a Difference in Our Community

QMRCC was founded with the mission to improve the lives of underserved populations through sustainable community development initiatives. We work collaboratively with local stakeholders to identify needs and develop appropriate solutions.

Our core values guide everything we do:

  • Compassion and empathy for those in need
  • Integrity and transparency in all operations
  • Community participation and inclusivity
  • Sustainable development approaches
